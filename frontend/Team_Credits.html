Stock Management, Smart Invoice & CRM System

A Comprehensive Solution for Inventory Management, Smart Invoicing and Customer Relationship Management

RiseTech Revolution CRM Software is an advanced business management solution designed to simplify and automate daily business operations through intelligent inventory control, smart invoicing, and customer relationship management.

The software is specially developed for wholesalers, retailers, distributors, trading companies, and service-oriented businesses that require an efficient, accurate, and user-friendly management system.

With an integrated platform, RiseTech Revolution CRM helps businesses maintain stock records, generate professional invoices, manage customer databases, track sales and purchases, monitor outstanding payments, and generate business reports — all from a single dashboard.

--- Key Features ---

  • Smart Invoice Generation with print and PDF export
  • Real-Time Stock & Inventory Management
  • Customer & Supplier Management
  • Purchase and Sales Tracking
  • Automated GST & Tax Calculations
  • Outstanding Payment Monitoring
  • Customer-wise & Date-wise Reports
  • Secure User Login & Access Control
  • Dashboard Analytics & Business Insights
  • Fast Search & Filter Options
  • Multi-User Support
  • Data Backup & Restore Facility
  • Professional and User-Friendly Interface

--- Benefits ---

  • Reduces manual paperwork and calculation errors
  • Improves business productivity and operational efficiency
  • Maintains accurate stock and financial records
  • Enhances customer relationship management
  • Provides quick access to invoices, reports, and transaction history
  • Saves time through automation and smart reporting tools
