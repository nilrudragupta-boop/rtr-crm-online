📚 Complete RTR-CRM User Manual & Functionality Guide

🔐 Login, Authentication & Security

Launch the application and enter your Username and Password.

  • Initial/default credentials are provided and authorized by the RTR-CRM Team.
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): If 2FA is enabled, you will be prompted for a 6-digit OTP code generated from your authenticator application.
  • Check "Remember Me" on trusted workstations to retain login sessions.
  1. Click "Forgot Password?" on the login screen.
  2. Enter the registered Recovery / Admin Email address.
  3. You will receive a 6-digit verification OTP in your email.
  4. Enter the OTP to verify your account and configure a new password.

Note: If you have not set up your SMTP or email settings yet, contact the RTR-CRM Team to update your recovery email address and credentials in system configurations.

When your license status indicates nearing expiry on the dashboard or startup banner:

  1. Click the Renew button on the dashboard or license screen.
  2. Enter your Admin ID and the official Renewal Code provided by the RTR-CRM Team.
  3. Specify the desired Duration (days) and subscription Amount.
  4. Click Request OTP. An authorization OTP is routed to the RTR-CRM Team to prevent unauthorized extensions.
  5. Click Make Payment to complete payment via UPI QR code or bank transfer.
  6. Enter the authorized verification OTP from the RTR-CRM Team to instantly activate and extend your system license validity.
🕹️ Dashboard, Navigation & Command Palette

The Dashboard displays real-time operational insights:

  • KPI Summaries: Total Sales, Purchases, Net Receivables, Payables, and Total Stock Valuation.
  • Trends: Sales vs. Purchases comparative chart across months.
  • Low Stock & Expiry Alerts: Quick overview of inventory items below reorder points or approaching expiry.
  • Privacy Mode (Eye Icon): Click the eye icon in the top header to mask sensitive financial amounts and profit figures when operating in public or customer-facing counters.

RTR-CRM includes high-speed navigation shortcuts:

  • Press Ctrl + K (or Cmd + K) anywhere to open the Global Command Palette. Quickly search pages, customer ledgers, item codes, and settings.
  • Favorites: Pin your most visited screens to the top of the left sidebar by clicking the ⚙️ gear icon next to Favorites.
  • Sidebar Search: Instantly filter navigation modules using the quick-filter bar on top of the menu.
📝 Invoicing, POS & Billing
  1. Navigate to Smart Invoice or Invoice.
  2. Select the Invoice Type:
    • Tax Invoice: Detailed B2B format containing GSTIN, CGST/SGST/IGST breakdown, and E-Way Bill fields.
    • Sale Invoice / Bill of Supply: Simplified retail invoice for B2C transactions or exempt goods.
    • Proforma Invoice: Preliminary bill before final dispatch and payment.
  3. Select the Customer (or type a new name to auto-create customer records).
  4. Scan barcodes or search items by name/code. HSN, unit price, and GST slab will auto-populate.
  5. Add item-level discounts, global discounts, or round-offs.
  6. Add extra charges such as Freight, Packaging, or Labor if applicable.
  7. Click Save & Print to commit inventory deduction and generate PDF printouts.

RTR-CRM includes a high-speed counter POS and table billing workflow:

  • POS Billing: Quick touch/barcode interface for retail and food service with single-click payment settlement.
  • Table & Order Sheets: Assign orders to designated dining tables, split bills, and add items incrementally.
  • Restaurant KDS (Kitchen Display System): Sent kitchen orders (KOT) update directly on the kitchen monitor screen in real-time, allowing cooks to mark items as "Preparing" or "Ready".

To settle invoices with mixed payments:

  1. Open the invoice in Invoice Report or during checkout.
  2. Click Split Payment / Edit Amount.
  3. Allocate amounts received in Cash and Bank / Online UPI.
  4. Select the destination ledger/bank account. The system automatically reconciles the invoice due balance and posts entries into the Banking ledger.
🛍️ Purchase & Supplier Management
  1. Go to Purchase Entries.
  2. Select the Supplier and enter their original vendor bill number and date.
  3. Add received items, cost rates, applicable tax rates, and batch numbers.
  4. Saving the purchase will automatically increase warehouse stock quantities and update average purchase prices.
  5. Outstanding dues are posted to the Supplier Ledger.

In Supplier Manager:

  • Store primary contact, billing address, and payment terms.
  • Click the 🔍 search icon next to the GSTIN input to perform live validation with the official GST registry.
📦 Inventory, Stock & Pharma Depository

Inventory is updated dynamically:

  • (+) Inflow: Purchase Entries, Sales Returns, Production Receipts, and Positive Stock Adjustments.
  • (-) Outflow: Sale Invoices, POS Bills, Purchase Returns, Scrap/Damages, and Raw Material Consumption.
  • Use Stock Ledger to audit chronological inward and outward movements for any specific item.

For pharmacies, healthcare, and expiring goods:

  • Track items by Batch Number, Manufacturing Date, and Expiry Date.
  • Assign shelf and Rack Locations for rapid warehouse picking.
  • The system raises visual alerts on the dashboard for batches expiring within 30, 60, or 90 days.
  • Stock Journal: Used for transferring stock across branches/godowns and manual physical count adjustments.
  • Scrap Inventory: Write off damaged, leaked, or expired stock with reason codes. Written-off stock is removed from sellable inventory and recorded as a business loss.
🏭 Manufacturing, Production & BOM

The manufacturing module streamlines assembly and recipe formulation:

  1. Create BOM (Bill of Materials): Define the finished product and specify required raw materials, packaging items, and overhead ratios per unit.
  2. Production Entry: Enter the quantity of finished goods produced.
  3. Automatic Consumption: RTR-CRM automatically deducts raw materials from inventory and adds finished units into stock with aggregated valuation.
  4. Review yields and losses via the Production Report.
💰 Finance, Banking & Ledger Management
  1. Open Ledger Report.
  2. Choose between Customer or Supplier.
  3. Select the party name and target date range.
  4. Inspect all debit/credit transactions, running balances, and export detailed PDF/Excel statements to share with parties.
  • Credit Note: Issued when customers return goods or when invoices are over-billed. It decreases the customer's balance.
  • Debit Note: Issued to suppliers when returning goods or receiving purchase rebates, decreasing payable liability.
  • Banking Module: Maintain multiple bank accounts, cash registers, and electronic wallets.
  • Contra Vouchers: Record internal cash deposits into banks or transfers between accounts.
  • Cheque Printing: Fill in payee names and amounts to format and print directly on physical bank cheque leaves.
🤝 Customer CRM & Business 360

Maintain client relationship pipelines:

  • Enquiries: Log inbound leads with source, requirements, and estimated deal value.
  • Follow-ups: Schedule reminder dates and times. View upcoming client touchpoints on the Reminders dashboard.
  • Customer Support: Log support tickets and track resolution statuses (Open, In-Progress, Resolved).

The Business 360 engine provides end-to-end intelligence:

  • Customer lifetime value (CLV) and churn prediction.
  • Top performing inventory items and high-margin product categories.
  • Supplier purchase trends and vendor lead times.
👥 HR & Staff Payroll
  • Staff Registry: Store employee profiles, role assignments, basic salary, and allowances.
  • Attendance Tracker: Record daily attendance (Present, Half-Day, Absent, Paid Leave).
  • Payroll Processing: Automatically compute monthly net pay, overtime, and deductions based on attendance logs and print salary slips.
📊 Reports & Tax Compliance
  • Day Book: Consolidated feed of all vouchers, sales, purchases, and receipts recorded on any single date.
  • Profit & Loss: Real-time gross and net profit analysis factoring COGS and operating expenses.
  • Balance Sheet: Structured view of Assets, Liabilities, and Equity balances.

Access the GST Return Report to review tax summaries:

  • GSTR-1: Outward supplies categorized by B2B (with GSTIN), B2C Large, and B2C Small.
  • GSTR-2: Inward purchases and input tax credit (ITC) breakdown.
  • GSTR-3B: Net payable tax summary after offsetting ITC against tax liability.
⚙️ Settings, PDF Studio & Backups

1. Local Backup: Click Backup Data on the dashboard. An encrypted .json backup file will be saved to your local storage.

2. Cloud Backup: Connect Google Drive storage in Settings. Backups will automatically sync to your cloud vault.

Note: If you need to re-link or authorize cloud storage credentials, contact the RTR-CRM Team for security token clearance.
