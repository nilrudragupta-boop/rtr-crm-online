RISE Tech Revolution - CRM

Overview and Functional Introduction for Operations, Reports, and Tools Modules

Operations

Smart Invoice

Advanced GST-compliant tax invoicing engine supporting multi-tier tax computations (CGST/SGST/IGST), reverse charges, itemized discounts, and printable output generation.

Regular Invoice

Alternative billing form designed for simplified retail billing, fee vouchers, or non-taxable service receipts.

Quick Daily Entry

High-speed, single-screen voucher entry module optimized for counter staff to rapidly record walk-in retail transactions without manual overhead.

Direct Sale Register

Rapid counter-sale logging tool that registers immediate inventory depletion and cash drawer additions.

Customer Sale

Credit and ledger-linked sales order portal that automatically updates individual party ledgers and receivable balances.

Quotation & Estimates

Generates commercial price estimates and formal proposals that can be directly converted into final tax invoices upon approval.

Purchase Entries

Procurement ledger for recording inward vendor bills, item inventory replenishment, Input Tax Credits (ITC), and Accounts Payable.

Returns Management

Processes incoming customer returns and outward vendor rejections, updating stock registers and party accounts.

Credit / Debit Notes

Issues formal Credit and Debit vouchers to rectify invoice discrepancies, post-sale discounts, or damaged inventory returns.

Production & BOM

Manufacturing assembly console allowing users to formulate Bills of Materials (BOM) and run batch jobs that consume raw items to yield finished goods.

Stock Journal

Facilitates internal warehouse transfers, wastage write-offs, physical inventory reconciliations, and ad-hoc stock entries/exits.

Order Sheet

Tracks fulfillment pipelines, backorders, and outstanding delivery obligations across clients and suppliers.

Restaurant POS & KDS

Touch-based food ordering terminal integrated with a live Kitchen Display System (KDS) showing active orders and preparation timers.

Master Directories

Central management desks for maintaining client profiles, vendor records, and inventory SKU items with HSN/SAC codes and default pricing.

Journal Voucher & Banking

Double-entry accounting journal for manual financial adjustments and a bank ledger tool to track check settlements and contra entries.

Reports & Analytics

Balance Sheet

Consolidated financial standing statement mapping total Liabilities, Owner's Capital, Fixed Assets, and Current Assets.

Profit & Loss Statement

Calculates Gross and Net Profit across chosen fiscal dates by aggregating trading turnover against operational and administrative costs.

GST Return Report

Formats B2B, B2C, HSN-wise, and tax-tier aggregates into standard summaries ready for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filings.

General Ledger

Chronological ledger statement showing debit/credit breakdowns, running balances, and opening balances for any specific account or client.

Day Book

Audit register listing every voucher, invoice, payment, and expense entry executed on any chosen calendar day.

Cash Flow Statement

Monitors liquid cash and bank inflows versus outflows across operating, investing, and financing business cycles.

Sales & Invoice Registers

Filterable transaction registers summarizing sales numbers, payment collection statuses, customer totals, and overdue receivables.

Purchase Report

Procurement dashboard tracking supplier expenditures, vendor credit aging, and item-wise inward volume.

Stock Report & Stock Ledger

Live inventory balances, reorder threshold alerts, batch movements, and valuation summaries for all warehouse SKUs.

Expense Manager

Categorizes operational overheads (wages, rent, utilities, delivery fees) under Indian accounting standard groups.

Production & Scrap Reports

Manufacturing audits tracking raw material utilization, batch yields, component wastage, and scrap inventory salvage.

Shadow Ledger

Internal verification ledger used to run secondary accounting and parallel bookkeeping.

Tools & Utilities

Business 360°

Consolidated enterprise dashboard delivering key performance indicators (KPIs), gross margins, top revenue sources, and inventory health metrics.

RTR PDF Studio

In-browser PDF toolkit to merge invoice batches, split multi-page contracts, rotate pages, and apply company watermarks.

Image-to-Text (OCR)

Client-side optical character recognition utility that extracts text and numerical tables directly from scanned physical receipts and vendor bills .

Cheque Print Tool

Pre-aligned cheque layout configurator that auto-converts numerical amounts to words and formats payee details for bank slips .

Tour Expense Manager

Field agent logging tool for tracking daily travel allowances, transport tickets, distance traveled, and customer visit expenses.

Follow-ups & Reminders

Automated alert system for payment collection calls, sales pipeline leads, overdue customer invoices, and task schedules.

Enterprise Chatter

Internal communication tool enabling direct messaging, team discussion channels, broadcast notices, and file attachments.

Email Sender

Email composition client supporting customizable templates and direct dispatch of invoice attachments to customers and vendors.

Custom Module Builders

Customised tools to define custom relational data tables, create metadata-driven forms, and add custom business fields to existing modules.
